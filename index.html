About Me

I'm Yuvraj Mohana.
I'm 18, and I ship things.

I've built a salon booking platform with a Supabase-powered admin dashboard, an AI-driven productivity workspace with Kanban boards and JWT auth, and a cybersecurity honeypot that uses Gemini to generate fake files that keep attackers engaged. This portfolio — with its GSAP scroll animations and custom cursor — is one of those projects too.

My stack leans React, TypeScript, and Python. I pick whatever gets the job done — Vite for fast builds, Supabase or PostgreSQL for data, Framer Motion or GSAP for animation. I like building things end-to-end: from database schema to deployed frontend.
